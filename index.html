Register if you dare…

Sign in at the door with your costume photo. Your photo and character go on the ballot, and your photo appears on the big screen if you win. Kids without a phone can register on a parent's phone.

Squad group photo

Groups and duos: snap one photo of the whole squad in costume. If your squad wins, this is the photo shown on the big screen. Anyone in the squad can add it or replace it.

Change my photo

Who's voting?

Tap your own photo to start. Sharing a phone? Take turns: pick yourself, vote, then pass the phone.

Cast your spell

Rank your top 3 per award: 🥇 1st gets 3 points, 🥈 2nd gets 2, 🥉 3rd gets 1. The most points wins. You can't vote for yourself, and you can change your votes until the host closes voting. Comments are optional and may be read out when the winners are revealed. 🕸️
